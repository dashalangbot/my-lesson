Iteration #2

Problem

  • People who moved to a new country struggle to communicate in real-life situations — work, doctors, housing, school, and daily errands.
  • Traditional ESL options are rigid, generic, and disconnected from learners' immediate goals and routines.
  • Learners want confidence and practical communication, but they freeze, avoid speaking, or can't find a format that fits their busy lives.
Existing alternatives

Duolingo, private tutors (Preply / SkyEng), free ESL classes, ChatGPT — abandoned for being too gamified, academic, slow, or disconnected from immigrant life.

Solution

  • AI-powered, personalized language learning app for adults who started over in a new country.
  • Daily, task-based lessons built around the learner's real life, goals, and routines.
  • Role-play simulations, spaced repetition, and immersive practice for functional communication and confidence-building.

Key Metrics

  • Weekly active learners
  • Lesson completion rate
  • Speaking practice frequency
  • Retention & churn
  • Progress in functional tasks — appointments, job communication, daily conversations

Unique Value Proposition

BeLang helps newcomers learn the language they need to finally feel at home — and reclaim their agency.

Combines personalization, cultural context, and AI practice to accelerate practical fluency and integration.

Real-life language for adults starting over in a new country.

Unfair Advantage

  • Built from 3.5+ years of direct private teaching with this audience — 200+ students, deep library of real immigrant scenarios.
  • Deep understanding of immigrant language barriers, motivation, and jobs-to-be-done.
  • Strong founder–market fit: Dasha personally experienced language and cultural barriers after moving to the U.S.

Channels

  • Direct-to-consumer — social media, content marketing, referral & community sharing.
  • Partnerships with immigrant support organizations, ESL providers, and newcomer communities.

Customer Segments

Beachhead

Russian-speaking non-native English speakers in North America with limited English proficiency.

Long-term market

Any country, any language — adult immigrants learning the language of their new home.

Early adopters

Women — stay-at-home or in low-wage jobs — who need English for daily life, work, and integration.

Cost Structure

  • Product development & maintenance
  • AI and hosting costs
  • Customer acquisition & community building
  • Support, operations & partnerships

Revenue Streams

  • Subscription-based B2C model
  • Freemium → paid conversion
  • Later: B2B / institutional partnerships with adult education organizations
  • Strong LTV from recurring use by adults needing ongoing language support
